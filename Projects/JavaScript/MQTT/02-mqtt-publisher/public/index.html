SCOPE

What it does

Publishes only — it never subscribes. A single publish is the default; bursts are explicit and bounded. The service binds to 127.0.0.1 unless HTTP_HOST says otherwise.

Only publish to brokers and topics you are authorized to use. Retained messages stay on the broker until replaced or cleared.
